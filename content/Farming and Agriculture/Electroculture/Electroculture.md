### Electricity meets the Ether; use metallic rods to amplify growth rates of crops

#### Basic Components
- [[Copper Wire]] (Create compat?)
- [[Zinc Wire]] (Create compat)
- **Sticks**

#### Rod Progression
1. [[Basic Rod]] - +10% growth rate
2. [[Twin Coil Rod]] - +25%
3. [[Auric Rod]] (**+** [[Gold Wire]]) - +50%
4. [[Verdant Rod]] - +100%
5. [[Etheric Rod]] - +150%
6. [[Luminal Rod]] - +200%
7. [[Elder Rod]] - +300%, occaisionally harvests crops within range
