#core/glow-utils 

Details:
- Infuses tools, armor, and special items with stored [[Glow]]
- Default capacity of **50,000 Glow**
- Send/receive speed of **150 Glow/s**
- Higher Glow charge give temporary buffs: Night Vision, Regeneration, or minor Levitation, among **many** others
