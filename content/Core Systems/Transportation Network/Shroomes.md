#core/teleportation

#### Details:
- Shroomes act as waypoints for teleportation
- They can teleport players between other Stumps and Shroomes
- If not connected by [[Roots]], each teleportation consumes **12,500** [[Glow]]
- Default capacity of **50,000 Glow** (~4 charges)
- Default recovery of **0.5 Glow/s**, including when underground
- Send/Receive speed of **150 Glow/s**
- Can be assigned colors for different biomes/dimensions
- **Personal Shroome**: A one-use Shroome that can be placed anywhere and is free to teleport within the dimension. Very hard to craft, and has a **10 minute** cooldown
