#core 

Glow is a natural, regenerating energy source found all throughout the [[Overworld]]. It powers transportation, storage, crafting, and growth. Measured in units of Glow, most devices send and receive at **150 Glow/s**.

[[Stumps]] and [[Shroomes]] can both **store** and **generate** Glow. [[Rootpools]] on the other hand, can simply **store** Glow. [[Roots]] can move Glow around, either to Shroomes or Stumps, or to other Devices such as the [[Glowlace Chamber]]
