*Piecing bits and pieces of things that are hidden in real life into an immersive and fully playable Minecraft mod*

### Essence of the Mod
*The Wanderwood* is a mod about connection. Ancient fungi, enchanted stumps, and glowing roots link the world together in an overhead and underground network of energy, called [[Glow]] Players can harness this to travel, cultivate strange soils, and grow an interconnected ecosystem of flora and fauna.
